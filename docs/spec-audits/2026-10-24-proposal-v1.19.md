### F-1 · drift · ADR-019 allows a dependency on an `invalidated` untouched task
- **Section:** Plan checks (`unknown-dependency`, line 579); "Spec diff and impact analysis" › Step 5 (line 863)
- **Evidence:** accepted ADR-019, Decision, "Plan checks": "In a replan `unknown-dependency` accepts untouched tasks that are not `abandoned`". The proposal (SC-189): "an untouched task that is neither `abandoned` nor `invalidated`". M6 item 5 builds this rule against two binding texts that disagree.
- **Proposed fix:** no spec change; the human adds an "## Amendment" to ADR-019 (as ADR-005 to ADR-007 have) before `apply_spec.sh 1.19`, and in any case before M6 item 5.

### F-2 · drift · code does not yet follow SC-190, SC-196, SC-200 and SC-202
- **Section:** "Spec diff and impact analysis" › Step 1 and Classifier checks; "Task state machine"
- **Evidence:** `src/openfactory/domain/spec_diff.py:84-95` makes a requirement deprecated in both versions `modified` when its hash or an ADR changed (SC-200: `unchanged`) and one deprecated only in `from` `modified`, criteria diffed against `from` (SC-202: `added`, against an empty side); `_cosmetic_with_work` in `src/openfactory/domain/classifier.py` and `src/openfactory/app/classifier_prompt.py:40` cover only a lost criterion (SC-190: lost or gained); `src/openfactory/app/approve_plan.py` has no `TRANSITIONS` check (SC-196).
- **Proposed fix:** change the code: M6 outline item 4a (SC-190, SC-200, SC-202) and item 7 (SC-196), both in `docs/progress.md`.

### F-3 · gap · is an ADR-only change `cosmetic`? (advisor)
- **Section:** "Spec diff and impact analysis" › Step 2 (the kind definitions, SC-201) and Step 1 (the ADR rule, SC-167)
- **Evidence:** "`cosmetic`: only the wording changed". A requirement whose own text is identical but whose ADR changed is `modified` and classified; nothing of it changed, so the definition does not say whether an ADR edit with no behavioural effect is `cosmetic`. SC-201 exists to pin eval ground truth before M7, and such an edit is a likely cosmetic eval case.
- **Proposed fix:** "`cosmetic`: only the wording of the requirement or of its ADRs changed; …", and the same words in `classifier_prompt.py`.

### F-4 · gap · `nothing to replan` for a changed requirement whose tasks are all `abandoned` (advisor)
- **Section:** "Spec diff and impact analysis" › Step 3 and Step 5; CLI commands (`replan`, line 926)
- **Evidence:** pre-existing. A `modified` requirement classified `behavioral` or `replacement` whose tasks are all `abandoned` gives no impacted task and is not `added`, so `replan` refuses with `nothing to replan` and its `new_work` (or a gained criterion) is never planned; the replan `uncovered` also counts the `abandoned` task as covering it. Same failure mode SC-202 fixed for an un-deprecated requirement. Not in the demo.
- **Proposed fix:** defer to Later; or count a seed with no live task as work for `nothing to replan` and the replan prompt.

### F-5 · wording · "not deprecated in `to`" in SC-202's sentence (advisor)
- **Section:** "Spec diff and impact analysis" › Step 1 (line 803)
- **Evidence:** "A requirement deprecated in `from` and not deprecated in `to` is `added`" could be stretched to a requirement absent from `to`; beside SC-194's explicit "absent or not deprecated" it plainly means present, and absence falls to `removed`.
- **Proposed fix:** "deprecated in `from` and present and not deprecated in `to`".

### F-6 · wording · the envelope rule does not list "last" (advisor)

## Advisor

### Q1: ADR-019 is accepted and says a replan's `unknown-dependency` accepts untouched tasks that are "not `abandoned`". v1.19 (SC-189) says "neither `abandoned` nor `invalidated`". Is that a real drift, and is it enough for the human to amend or annotate ADR-019 before v1.19 is applied, with no spec change?
- Proposed: No spec change; the human amends or annotates ADR-019 to match before applying v1.19.
- Challenge: The drift is real. ADR-019 Decision, "Plan checks", says `unknown-dependency` "accepts untouched tasks that are not `abandoned`", so a dependency on an `invalidated` untouched task is allowed. The proposal's Plan checks row (line 579) and replan prompt (line 863) forbid it. CLAUDE.md makes both the spec and accepted ADRs binding, so M6 item 5, which builds exactly this rule, would face two binding sources that disagree. The spec is right on the merits: an `invalidated` task never passes, so a new task depending on it stays `pending` for ever. That is why the fix belongs in the ADR, not the spec. The proposal assumes "before applying" is a hard gate. The ADR-014 Consequences paragraph shows a precedent of letting an ADR lag ("ADR-005's amendment lags the spec ... until the human amends it"). Here a lag would land on the very next coding task, so the gate is justified. It should be an amendment, not just a note in decisions.md: ADR-005, ADR-006 and ADR-007 already carry "## Amendment" sections. I checked the other v1.19 changes against ADR-019 and found no further conflict: SC-190 (ADR-019 names the checks only), SC-196, SC-200 and SC-202 ("newly deprecated requirements are decided by rules").
- Strongest alternative: A new ADR that supersedes only the "Plan checks" clause of ADR-019. It is cleaner for the history but heavier, and the in-place amendment precedent already exists.
- Conflicts: ADR-019 (accepted), Decision, "Plan checks", against proposal "Plan checks" › `unknown-dependency` and "Step 5: replan". It also narrows the 2026-10-24 SC-163 row, which SC-189 says openly.
- Recommendation: Do as proposed: the human adds an "## Amendment" to ADR-019 (an agent may draft the text) before `apply_spec.sh 1.19`, and in any case before M6 item 5 starts. This follows the existing amendment precedent and is the smallest fix.
- Verdict: AGREE

### Q2: The code does not yet follow the new Step 1, classifier and approve-plan rules. Is that a real gap, and is it right to fix it in code through M6 items 4a and 7?
- Proposed: Change the code: M6 outline item 4a (diff and classifier rules) and item 7 (approve plan), already in docs/progress.md.
- Challenge: The evidence holds.
  - `diff_specs` (spec_diff.py lines 84-95): a requirement deprecated in both versions with a changed hash skips the `deprecated` branch and stays `modified`. With an unchanged hash and a changed ADR it becomes `modified` through the ADR branch. SC-200 says `unchanged`.
  - A requirement deprecated only in `from` has a different hash, so it is `modified`, and its criteria are diffed against `before`. SC-202 says `added`, diffed against an empty side.
  - `_cosmetic_with_work` checks only `_lost`, and the prompt's last rule (classifier_prompt.py line 40) covers only a lost criterion. SC-190 also needs a gained one.
  - approve_plan.py has no `TRANSITIONS` check (SC-196), and none of the replan path (the impact read, the refusal, the invalidations).

  The spec wins over code, so a code fix is right. Where the plan falls short is the bookkeeping:
  - Item 7's text names only SC-165 and SC-176, not SC-196 (the `TRANSITIONS` check), SC-192 (refusal order) or SC-195. Yet the SC-196 row says "built in M6 item 7".
  - Item 6 does not name SC-197 (where `replan` writes its warning).
  - The SC-190, SC-200 and SC-202 rows say "code follow-up in M6 item 1c", but progress.md calls that work item 4a.

  As written, an implementer could build item 7 without the `TRANSITIONS` check.
- Strongest alternative: Same code fix, but first add SC-192, SC-195 and SC-196 to item 7's line, add SC-197 to item 6's line, and change "item 1c" to "item 4a" in the SC-190, SC-200 and SC-202 rows. This is a doc-only refinement, not a different fix.
- Conflicts: None for the proposed fix. The current code conflicts with proposal "Step 1", "Classifier checks" › `cosmetic-with-work`, and "Task state machine" ("The `run`, `resolve` and `approve plan` use cases check every transition against `TRANSITIONS`"). That is expected until items 4a and 7.
- Recommendation: Fix the code in items 4a and 7 as proposed, and fix the progress.md and decisions.md cross-references in the same spec commit. The spec wins over code, and the outline should name every rule each item must build.
- Verdict: AGREE

### Missed findings (changed sections and what they rely on)
- **M-1 · gap · SC-201 "Step 2" kind definitions against SC-167.** `cosmetic` is defined as "only the wording changed". Under Step 1's last rule, a requirement whose own text is identical but whose ADR changed counts as `modified` and is classified. Nothing in it changed, so "only the wording changed" does not say whether an ADR-only edit with no behavioural effect is `cosmetic`. SC-201 exists to pin the eval ground truth before M7 labelling, and an ADR-only edit is a likely eval case among the "2 cosmetic". Suggest one clause, for example "only the wording of the requirement or of its ADRs changed". If the clause goes in, the prompt text in classifier_prompt.py needs the same wording.
- **M-2 · wording · SC-202 against SC-194, "Step 1".** SC-194 says "absent or not deprecated in `from`" explicitly. SC-202 says "deprecated in `from` and not deprecated in `to`", which a literal reader could stretch to cover a requirement absent from `to`, making it `added`. Read beside SC-194 it plainly means present and not deprecated, and absent falls to the default `removed`. Writing "present and not deprecated in `to`" would close the gap. Low.
- **M-3 · checked, no contradiction · Step 1 rules against Step 3 seeds, `nothing to replan`, `uncovered`, the ImpactComputed paragraph and criterion diffing.**
  - SC-200: a requirement deprecated in both versions is left out of `ImpactComputed.requirements` ("not unchanged"), is no seed, and `uncovered` never demands it.
  - SC-202: an `added` requirement is not a seed. The old tasks of a requirement deprecated in `from` were already invalidated when the replan for that deprecation was approved, so they are not live. The requirement counts against `nothing to replan` and must be covered by a new task. This is consistent.
  - The criteria of a requirement deprecated in both versions may still diff as changed, but nothing reads criterion changes except for `modified` requirements (the `obsolete_criteria` paragraph and `cosmetic-with-work`), so no behaviour depends on it.
  - SC-194's new-and-already-deprecated requirement is a seed with no linked tasks, which is harmless.
